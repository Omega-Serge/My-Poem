A JOURNEY THROUGH 12 ERAS

From Debut, where dreams began to bloom,
To Fearless, chasing stars beyond the moon.
Through Speak Now, where silent hearts found words,
And Red, where love was felt in every verse.

In 1989, we danced beneath the city lights,
While Reputation taught us to reclaim our fights.
With Lover, came colors, warmth, and grace,
And folklore left stories time cannot erase.

Through evermore, where memories remain,
Midnights held our thoughts, our hopes, our pain.
With The Tortured Poets Department, ink met the soul,
And that is how The Life of a Showgirl took its final bow.

Twelve albums, twelve chapters, one story untold,
Growing, dreaming, and being brave and bold.
For every era, a piece of who we are,
12 different worlds, united by one destined star.
